Our Story

Delving into the architectural philosophy and vision behind the creation of FONTDALE.

An Oasis Amidst the City Hustle

Conceptualized in 2023 and officially opened in 2025, FONTDALE was born from the visionary mind of Franz Diego. Operating on the premise that urban society needs more than just a transactional space, FONTDALE was built with an architectural approach that prioritizes natural light, air circulation, and green landscape integration.

Our structure is divided into two main wings. The North Gallery is dedicated to the exploration of fashion and dynamic lifestyles, while the South Pavilion offers a more relaxed retail and dining experience. Both are harmoniously stitched together by our architectural masterpiece, the Lumen Skywalk—a glass suspension bridge offering a new perspective of the city.

Today, FONTDALE at Arcadia Circle is not merely a stopover, but the new heartbeat for a modern community that values aesthetics, quality, and togetherness.
